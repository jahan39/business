Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery generally takes 1–2 days. Express and same-day options may be available.

Can I customize a cake?

Yes. Use our custom cake request form and tell us your preferred design.

What payment methods are supported?

Bangladesh payment options include bKash, Nagad, Rocket and SSLCommerz. International checkout can use Stripe when connected to the backend.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Logged-in customers can view order status and tracking.
